RECIPE: Simple Tomato Pasta
servings: 2

Ingredients:
pasta 200g
tomatos 4
garlic 2 cloves
olive oil 2 tablespoons
salt
basil

Steps:
Boil water in a large pot and add salt.
Cook the pasta for 10 minutes.
Chop the tomatos and the garlic.
Fry the garlic in olive oil for 1 minute.
Add the tomatos and cook for 10 minutes.
Mix the pasta with the sauce and add basil.
Serve hot. Enjoy your meal!!
