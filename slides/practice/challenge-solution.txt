RECIPE: Simple Tomato Pasta
Servings: 4

Ingredients:
- pasta 200g
- tomatoes 4
- garlic 2 cloves
- olive oil 2 tablespoons
- salt
- black pepper
- basil

Steps:
1. Boil water in a large pot and add salt.
2. Cook the pasta for 12 minutes.
3. Chop the tomatoes and the garlic.
4. Fry the garlic in olive oil for 1 minute.
5. Add the tomatoes and cook for 10 minutes.
6. Mix the pasta with the sauce and add basil.
7. Serve hot. Enjoy your meal!
