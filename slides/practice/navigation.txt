The Pacific Ocean is the largest and deepest ocean on Earth.
It covers about one third of the surface of the planet.
Many islands are scattered across it, including Hawaii and Fiji.

Ferdinand Magellan named it "Mar Pacifico", meaning peaceful sea.
Despite the name, it is home to powerful storms called typhoons.
The Mariana Trench, its deepest point, is nearly 11,000 meters deep.

Scientists estimate that more than 80 percent of the ocean is unexplored.
New species are discovered in its depths almost every year.
Protecting the Pacific is important for the health of the whole planet.
